## Design reading

- **D1 §31.3 / §12.2.** Wire bytes for a mutation stream are a property of the stream, not of
  rendering. That is why the server-to-client payload can be measured with a minimal replica.
- **D1 §31.4.** RTT is injected at 0/100/300/600 ms. Server-dependent latency is measured apart
  from local interaction.
- **D1 §32.** The conformance suites are unchanged; nothing in the protocol or runtime moved.
- **D2 T34.** The §31 benchmarks and their report in `benchmarks/` are generic, and PX-027
  leaves them unchanged. It measures srtop's own deployment over real SSH, as PX-025 measured it
  behind the bridge.
- **H1.** htop is the comparison baseline. Debian 12 packages 3.2.2-2, and that is what was
  measured. H1's latest is 3.5.3, and that difference is recorded as a gap.
- **Range requests (D1 §§8, 22.7).** srtop publishes every row it holds and installs no range
  provider (PX-018's note; `model_range.rs`). A `CLIENT_MODEL_RANGE_REQUEST` for rows the session
  already pushed is answered `AlreadyCached`, and nothing is sent.
  - "Bounded progress" for srtop therefore means that the requested window keeps receiving
    authoritative rows under churn and RTT.
  - A request has made progress when the replica holds a revision committed after the request
    reached srtop, holds every row of the window, and those rows are an undelayed replica's at
    that revision.
  - Making progress here needs no generic facility, so none was added.

## Limitations and follow-ups

- **htop's version.** It is 3.2.2-2, Debian 12's, not H1's 3.5.3.
- **Matched comparisons.** There is no matched comparison above 210 processes, or at an interval
  other than 1 s. The large-table regime is measured on the SRUI side only.
- **The link.** No bandwidth cap or packet loss was imposed. RTT was added in userspace on
  loopback, through Docker Desktop's port forward.
- **The measuring client** is the minimal replica, not the AppKit client. The server's stream does
  not depend on the client, and the native client sends nothing in the steady state (PX-025).
- **lc-debt.** The TCP relay's accept loop polls every 5 ms, so that `stop` never waits on a
  blocked accept (`traffic.rs`).
- **Pinning.** The devbox's apt packages other than htop are still not pinned.
- **The walkthrough addition** needs Rust in the clean account, and the user must opt in with
  the flag.
